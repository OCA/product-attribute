Configure your groups under ??? menu ???
You have to choose:

* A name for the group
* Related product attribute
* Your sub-selection of values for that group

Once the group is ready, you can configure your
porduct template. On each product template
attribute line you will find this new field that
will change dynamically your selection of values.

If you add manually a new value on your product
template attribute line, the group will be reset.
