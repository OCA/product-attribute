This module shows a unit price (price per reference unit of measure) on
product labels, so customers can compare products sold in different
package sizes, as required by EU Directive 98/6/EC on consumer protection
in the indication of the prices of products.

The reference unit of measure is chosen freely and may belong to a
different category than the product's unit of measure: a product sold per
Units can show its price per kg or per L. The ratio is the quantity of the
reference unit contained in one unit of the product (its net content), so
it depends on the product, not on the conversion factors of the units of
measure.

Example, two products sold per Units:

- Tomato paste 500 g: $2.50 per Units, ratio 0.5 → $5.00 per kg
- Tomato paste 1 kg: $4.50 per Units, ratio 1 → $4.50 per kg
