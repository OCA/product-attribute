Odoo only enforces that a lot or serial number is unique per product, so the
same number can be given to two different products. That is deliberate: vendors
choose their own numbering and two of them may collide.

It is the wrong answer when the numbers are meant to identify a product on their
own, for instance when one sequence numbers everything a company manufactures.
This module refuses a lot or serial number another product of the same company
already holds.
