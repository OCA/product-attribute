This module adds the use of the ``product_state`` module in Invoicing.

It adds a *Can Be Invoiced* flag on product states. Products whose state
does not allow invoicing are hidden from the product selection on
customer invoices, credit notes and receipts, and adding such a product
to an invoice line is refused. Vendor bills are not restricted. The flag
is exposed on the product as *State Allows Invoicing*. The check uses
the state of the product variant, so variant-specific states (e.g. with
``product_status``) are honoured.

Usage
=====

To prevent products of a state from being invoiced:

1. Open the product state. With ``product_state_sale`` installed, states
   are listed under *Sales > Configuration > Products > Product States*.
2. Untick *Can Be Invoiced*.
3. Products in this state no longer appear in the product selection of
   customer invoices, credit notes and receipts, and cannot be set on
   invoice lines. This also applies to invoices generated from sales
   orders. Vendor bills are not restricted. Existing invoice lines are
   not modified.

.. image:: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OCA/product-attribute/19.0/product_state_account/readme/static/description/product_state_form.png
   :alt: Product state form with the Can Be Invoiced toggle

The flag is enabled by default, so existing states keep their behaviour
until you change them. The list view shows the flag for all states:

.. image:: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OCA/product-attribute/19.0/product_state_account/readme/static/description/product_state_list.png
   :alt: Product state list with the Can Be Invoiced column

Editing states requires the *Product State Manager* group.
