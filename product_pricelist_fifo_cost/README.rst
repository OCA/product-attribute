In Odoo 18, the *Cost* of a product valued in FIFO is the average cost
of what is left in stock. Pricing on that cost no longer follows the
lots actually sold: with 20 units bought at 10 and 20 at 15, the cost is
12.5, while the next 20 units sold cost 10.

This module adds a new "Based on" option to pricelist rules: **FIFO Cost
(oldest lot in stock)**. The base price is the unit cost of the oldest
stock valuation layer with quantity left, that is the lot a FIFO
delivery takes next. When nothing is in stock, the product cost is used,
as with the *Cost* base.

Usage
=====

1. Use the FIFO costing method on the product category.
2. Go to *Sales > Products > Pricelists*, add a rule with *Price Type* =
   *Formula* and *Based on* = *FIFO Cost (oldest lot in stock)*.
3. Set the markup, for example 50% to sell at 1.5 times the cost of the
   lot being sold.

With 20 units bought at 10 and 20 at 15, the product sells at 15 until
the first 20 units leave the stock, then at 22.50.
