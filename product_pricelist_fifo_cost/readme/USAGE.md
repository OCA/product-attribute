1. Use the FIFO costing method on the product category.
2. Go to *Sales > Products > Pricelists*, add a rule with *Price Type* =
   *Formula* and *Based on* = *FIFO Cost (oldest lot in stock)*.
3. Set the markup, for example 50% to sell at 1.5 times the cost of the lot
   being sold.

With 20 units bought at 10 and 20 at 15, the product sells at 15 until the
first 20 units leave the stock, then at 22.50.
