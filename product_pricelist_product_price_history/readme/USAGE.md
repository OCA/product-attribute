1. Automatic Tracking You don’t need to do anything! Every time you change a "Fixed Price" in any pricelist, the system automatically saves the old price, the new price, and the person who made the change.

2. View the History Go to Sales > Products > Pricelist History. Here you will see a list of all recent price adjustments.

3. Print Labels for New Prices 

* Open the Pricelist History list.

* Select the products (tick the checkboxes) that have new prices.

* Click the "Print Labels" button that appears in the top header.
