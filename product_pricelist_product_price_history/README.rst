This module provides an automated audit trail for price changes across
all product pricelists. It tracks modifications to fixed prices,
capturing the previous and new values, the user responsible, and the
timestamp. It includes a dedicated view for managers to review pricing
trends and a bulk-action feature to print product labels immediately
after price adjustments.

Usage
=====

1. Automatic Tracking You don’t need to do anything! Every time you
   change a "Fixed Price" in any pricelist, the system automatically
   saves the old price, the new price, and the person who made the
   change.

2. View the History Go to Sales > Products > Pricelist History. Here you
   will see a list of all recent price adjustments.

3. Print Labels for New Prices

- Open the Pricelist History list.

- Select the products (tick the checkboxes) that have new prices.

- Click the "Print Labels" button that appears in the top header.
